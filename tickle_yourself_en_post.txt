Why you can't tickle yourself (your brain is doing it on purpose) 🧠

Your brain predicts your own touch and turns it down. Scientists proved it with a tickle robot, and the exceptions are fascinating.

Sources:
Blakemore, Wolpert & Frith (2000) NeuroReport
Blakemore et al. (1999) Journal of Cognitive Neuroscience
Blakemore et al. (2000) Psychological Medicine
