This tiny animal survived space 🐻🚀

Tardigrades (water bears) survived 12 days of open space, inspired a protein that protects human cells, and crash-landed on the Moon.

Sources:
Jönsson et al. (2008) Current Biology
Hashimoto et al. (2016) Nature Communications
Traspas & Burchell (2021) Astrobiology
