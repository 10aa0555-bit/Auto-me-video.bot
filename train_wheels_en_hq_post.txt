Trains don't have steering wheels. So how do they turn? 🚂

Train wheels are very slightly cone-shaped. On a curve the wheelset slides outward, the outer wheel rolls on a bigger circle than the inner one, and the solid axle steers itself around the bend. The flanges are mostly a backup.
