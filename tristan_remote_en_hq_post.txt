The loneliest island on Earth 🌊 #geography #maps

About 250 people live on Tristan da Cunha in the South Atlantic: about 2,400 km from Saint Helena, 2,800 km from South Africa, with no airport. In 1961 the whole island was evacuated after an eruption, and most islanders came back two years later.
