Why you forget why you walked into a room 🚪🤔

It might be the door's fault. Scientists found that walking through a doorway makes you forget more than walking the same distance in one room.

Source: Radvansky, Krawietz & Tamplin (2011) Quarterly Journal of Experimental Psychology
